Why Emera exists

AI is breaking the internet. We are using it to build the fix

Fake photos, cloned voices, bot crowds and stolen work now spread faster than anyone can check them. The people who make things lose the credit, everyone else loses track of what is real, and the outcry keeps growing while little changes. The same AI that floods the internet lets a small team build the answer. We refuse to believe a fair, honest place to talk and share is out of reach, so we are building one in the open, for every human being.

You can't tell what is real

AI makes a fake photo, video or voice in seconds, and it looks just like the real thing.

On EmeraEvery post carries a signed record of who shared it and when, and the badge says only what was checked.

Bots talk louder than people

Bot accounts, bought followers and machine-written replies drown out the humans.

On EmeraPeople are marked, bots say what they are, and the verified human mark is free for everyone.

What you make gets taken

Copies spread faster than credit, and the person who made it is the last to hear about it.

On EmeraCopies point back to the original, and we help you take down the ones you find.

Rules nobody can read

A hidden ranking decides who gets seen, and it too often rewards what pushes people apart.

On EmeraThe ranking code is public, and every post can tell you why you are seeing it.

AI is destroying the internet, for the people who create and for everyone who reads, watches and listens. The outcry is already beyond belief.

But AI also lets us build the solutions. I refuse to believe there is no way to give people a fair, authentic place to express themselves and talk to each other. So we are building it.

Roy, founder of Emera

Our missionA fair, authentic place on the internet for every human being, where the people are real, what you share keeps your name on it, and the rules are written where everyone can read them.
