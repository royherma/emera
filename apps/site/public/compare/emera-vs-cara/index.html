Emera vs Cara

Short answerCara is a live, artist-focused app with AI filtering and portfolio features; Emera is a new, open-source nonprofit network where every post carries a signed record of who shared it and when. Choose Cara for an art community today, Emera for provenance and account portability.

Where the others are the better pick

  • Cara is live today as an artist-first community, while Emera is new.
  • Cara filters AI-generated images from portfolios with a built-in detector, which many artists want as a first-day feature.
  • Cara has job listings from studios, direct messages, bookmarks and an About page for longer bios.
  • Cara has an iOS app and a website that artists can use right now.

What Cara is

Cara is an image-sharing app and website for artists, created by Jingna Zhang, who runs it with a group of volunteers. It grew out of artists' concern about AI-generated art on larger platforms. Its App Store listing says an AI detector filters AI images out of portfolios, and it also applies NoAI tags to art as a voluntary signal to AI scrapers.

Day to day it works as both a portfolio and a feed. You can customize your home feed, set up custom Explore tabs, share images, GIFs and embedded videos, use direct messages and bookmarks, and browse job listings. The iOS app is free, with optional tips and supporter tiers from the developer, Cara Project, Inc.

What Emera is

Emera is an open-source nonprofit network on AT Protocol for everyone who makes things. Every post carries a signed record (C2PA content credentials) of who shared it and when it went up, and a Signed badge says exactly what was checked and nothing more. People are marked, and automated accounts are labeled as automated. Reposts point back to the original post.

Found a copy of your work elsewhere? You report it, Emera helps you file the takedown, and every step shows on your dashboard. Because you own your account on AT Protocol, you can move it with your followers to another host.

How to choose

If you want an art community you can join today, with an AI-image filter and a portfolio built in, Cara is the stronger fit. If you care about a signed record on each post, takedown help for copies, and an account you can carry to another host, Emera is built around those. Many artists will reasonably have a profile on both.

Emera is new. We do not describe it as bigger or more established than Cara, because it is not.

People also ask

Is Emera a Cara alternative?

It can be a second home for your work. Both welcome artists, but they focus on different things: Cara on an art community with AI filtering, Emera on signed posts, takedown help and account portability.

Does Cara allow AI art?

Cara says it will not host AI-generated portfolios until ethical and data privacy issues are resolved through regulation, and it uses a detector to filter AI images from portfolios.

Who runs Cara?

Founder Jingna Zhang runs Cara with a group of volunteers, and the App Store lists the developer as Cara Project, Inc. She has said she avoided venture funding on purpose.

Can I move my account from Emera?

Yes. Emera is built on AT Protocol, so you own your account and can move it with your followers to another host.
