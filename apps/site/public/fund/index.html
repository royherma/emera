The creators people watch get half of every sponsor dollar

People who create get copied, faked and paid in exposure. Emera pays them. Companies sponsor Emera, half of every dollar goes to the creators people watched that month, half keeps Emera running, and every dollar in and out is public.

Your share is your watch time

Each month the creator half is shared by watch time. If 2% of the month's counted watch time was on your posts, you get 2% of the creator pool.

Example
Creator pool$5,000
Runs Emera$5,000
You get$100

Your amount = creator pool x your share of counted watch time, rounded down to the cent. Leftover cents roll into next month's pool.

What counts as a watched second
  • Only signed-in viewers count, so a crowd of logged-out bots adds nothing.
  • Your own views never count.
  • One viewer counts for at most 60 seconds on a photo post and 10 minutes on a video post each day.
  • One viewer counts for at most 30 minutes across one creator's posts each day, so no superfan or farm account decides who gets paid.
  • Accounts younger than 24 hours count zero.
  • A post taken down as a copy earns nothing, and its seconds go back to everyone else.

Money can't buy reach here

Sponsors back what people create. They get their name in daylight and a statement of where every dollar went. They never get a say in what anyone sees.

What a sponsor gets

  • A one-line banner marked Sponsor at the top of the app feed
  • Your name and link on this page, sorted by amount
  • Each month's statement showing where your money went, creator by creator

What a sponsor never gets

  • A boost for any post, or a say in ranking, moderation or takedowns
  • Targeting, a badge, or any data about who watched what

Sponsor cards between posts start once Emera has 1,000 posts, one after every 12, always marked Sponsor. Until then the first sponsors get the feed banner.
