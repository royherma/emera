# Design System: Emera Twilight

Source of truth for the tokens is `packages/brand/tokens.json` (generated by `packages/brand/build-tokens.mjs`, which also fails the build if any text color drops under 4.5:1). The app reads them through `packages/brand/tokens.ts` and the theme hook in `apps/mobile/src/state/theme.tsx`. Hex values are in `tokens.json` beside each OKLCH value; React Native has no OKLCH, so the app uses the hex.

## Overview

**Creative north star: The Horizon.** The founder picked Twilight from the directions in `brand/directions/full-twilight.html`. The page is cream paper by day and black by night, and the work is the only loud thing on it. One sun orange marks the one thing to press, the one place that is active, and the signature on every piece. Everything else is warm neutral, so the colors drawn from each creator's own work (rings, chips, swatches) are the color of the product.

- Mode: Operate. Phone first, one hand, short sessions. Desktop is a sidebar plus one 560px column.
- Day and night are equal citizens. Day is cream paper (never white), night is near black (never pure). Auto follows the system and the choice is saved in settings.
- Density: dense feed rows, 12px to 16px gutters, 3px gaps in the profile grid, 5px in the explore bento.
- Voice: welcoming. No rival is ever named. Copy rules from `CLAUDE.md` apply (see Do's and Don'ts).

## Colors

All colors are defined per theme as OKLCH in the frontmatter and in `tokens.json`. Use roles, not values.

| Role | Day | Night | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Paper (`bg`) | cream | black | Screen background |
| Surface | warm white | raised black | Cards, tab bar, sheet, proof block |
| Sunk | deeper cream | raised black+ | Fields, chips, secondary buttons, skeletons |
| Ink / Ink 2 / Ink 3 | brown black / mid / quiet | cream / mid / quiet | Text. Ink 3 is the lowest text tier and still passes 4.5:1 |
| Sun | orange `oklch(74.9% 0.173 54)` | same | Primary action, active dot, caret, focus ring, selection |
| Sun high | gold `oklch(84.9% 0.149 81)` | same | Top of the sun gradient, the Signed shield |
| Sun ink | burnt orange | pale orange | Orange used as text or icon on tinted ground |
| On sun | near black | same | Text on the sun gradient |
| Hair | 10% brown | 10% cream | 1px dividers and outlines |
| Danger | red | light red | Errors and Delete only |

**Creator colors.** Twelve pairs (`colors.creator` in tokens). A creator gets one pair by hashing their handle. The pair is the avatar ring gradient, the category dot, the profile highlight ring and the soft wash at the top of their profile. In Explore, "Colors of today" is the set of today's creator colors and each swatch filters the feed. Where a creator's real media is available a later build can pull the pair from their own work; the slot stays the same.

**Rules.** Sun covers at most about 10% of a screen. A screen never has two sun gradient buttons. Tints are made by alpha of the role color (`alpha(c.sun, 0.16)`), never by a new hue. Night is not day inverted: surfaces step up in lightness, shadows get stronger, and the sun text role switches to Sun ink.

## Typography

Sora for headings and numbers, DM Sans for everything small. Both are loaded with `expo-font` from `@expo-google-fonts` (Sora 400/600/700, DM Sans 400/500/600/700).

## Layout

- Spacing scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64. Page gutter 16 on phone, 32 on desktop.
- Breakpoint: 900px. Below it the screen is a single column with a floating tab bar. At 900px and above it is a 240px sidebar and a 560px feed column (profile and explore use the same column).
- Feed row, top to bottom: avatar row (44px tall, avatar 38), media, caption (2 lines), category tag. Rows are 24px apart.
- Profile: avatar 84 and counts, name and bio, actions, highlights, hairline, 3-up grid with 3px gaps.
- Explore: search, category chips, Colors of today, bento (3 columns, a 2x2 and a 1x2 tile, then a row of three, flipping each block).
- Content clears the tab bar (64px plus the bottom inset plus 44px).

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default. Depth comes from tonal layers (paper, surface, sunk) and one soft shadow each for the three things that float: the tab bar, post media, the action sheet. Shadows always carry an offset and a negative spread (`0 12 26 -10`). The sun button carries a warm shadow. Nothing else casts one.

**Glass.** Used only for small chips that sit on top of media (Signed mark, 1/3 index, play button, tile badges), always over a dark scrim. On web it adds `backdrop-filter: blur(10px) saturate(1.5)` only when `CSS.supports` says it is available, and the scrim gets lighter to match. Everywhere else (native, older browsers) it is the solid 66% scrim with the same 1px light outline. No other surface uses blur: the tab bar, sheet, headers and cards are solid. The profile wash is a gradient, not a blurred image.

## Shapes

Radii nest: tile 12 (grid), field 14, button 13, proof 16, card 18, media 20, sheet and tab bar 24, pill for chips, avatars and marks. A child inside a padded parent takes the parent radius minus the padding. Avatars are circles with a 2.2px gradient ring and a 2px paper gap. Icons are 24px line icons at 1.7 stroke (2 when active), round caps, drawn in `Icon.tsx`.

## Motion

Press 90ms (scale 0.98), fast 160ms, base 220ms, sheet 280ms, all with ease-out exponential (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`). The one authored moment is the action sheet rising over a fading scrim. Skeletons pulse between 55% and 100% opacity over 1.8s. Nothing loops except skeletons and the refresh spinner. Respect reduced motion on native by letting the OS setting skip the pulse (follow-up).

## Components

- **Post row.** Header (ring avatar, name, `@handle · time`, "..." button), media at radius 20 with the Signed mark top-left and the index chip top-right when there are several items, then caption (handle in 600) and the category tag (creator color dot with a 25% halo, label in ink 2). The "..." opens the action sheet. No counts or buttons that the backend can't honor.
- **Grid tile.** Radius 12 (bento 16). Bottom-right 20px glass shield; top-right layers badge when a post has several items; video shows a glass play disc. Press scales to 0.98.
- **Signed mark.** Glass pill, gold shield, "Signed" in white 11px label. The compact form is a 20px circle with only the shield. It appears on every piece of media. It means the file carries a signature and nothing more.
- **Proof block.** Surface card (day) or 12% sun wash with a 20% sun outline (night), radius 16. A 38px sun gradient seal, then "Made by {name}", "Signed {date and time}", fingerprint. It never says "verified human" or "original", because the app only knows who signed and when. No buttons live in it.
- **Action sheet.** Bottom sheet, radius 24, grabber, rows 52px tall (icon 20 plus label), Cancel row in sunk. Order: Report a copy, Share, Copy link, then Delete post (own posts, danger). Scrim 55% black, tap or Escape closes. On desktop it is a centered 440px card. "Report a copy" lives here and nowhere else on the post; it opens `/report`.
- **Tab bar.** Floating, 14px from the edges, 64px tall, radius 24, surface fill, 1px hair outline, soft shadow. Home and Explore left, a 46px sun gradient circle with a plus in the center, Profile (own avatar) right. Active tab is ink with a 5px sun dot under it. On desktop it becomes the sidebar with the Day / Night / Auto control at the bottom.
- **Profile header.** Ring avatar 84, Works and Joined counts, name (Sora 19), `@handle`, bio. Actions are Follow (sun, flex 1.3), Message (sunk), Share (icon button). On your own profile: Share profile and Settings. No Tip button, ever. Highlights are 56px rings (Photos, Films, Series) that filter the grid.
- **Chips.** 30px tall pill, sunk fill, creator color dot, label 12.5px. Selected chips are ink fill with paper text.
- **Buttons.** Primary (sun gradient, on-sun text, sun shadow), secondary (sunk), ghost (transparent), danger (sunk with danger text). Heights 36, 44, 52. One primary per screen. Disabled is 45% opacity.
- **Fields.** 52px, sunk fill, 14px radius, no border at rest, 1.5px sun border on focus, danger border and a meta-size message on error. Labels are 11px uppercase ink 2.
- **Empty, loading and error states.** Skeleton rows and grids match the final layout. Empty states have a 64px sun-tint disc with a line icon, an h2 title, one sentence, and the single action that fills the state. Errors name the problem and offer Try again.
- **Segmented control.** Day, Night, Auto in a sunk pill; the selected segment is ink with paper text. Lives in Settings and the desktop sidebar.
- **Toast.** Ink pill above the tab bar with a sun check, 2.2 seconds.

## Do's and Don'ts

Do
- Put the sun on exactly one thing per screen.
- Let creator colors carry the personality; keep the chrome warm and quiet.
- Keep "Report a copy" in the "..." menu: available, quiet.
- Pair every glass surface with a solid fallback and keep it small and over media.
- Show empty, loading and error states for every list.
- Give every icon button a 44px target and an accessibility label.
- Read all colors, fonts and radii from `tokens.json`; add a token before adding a value.

Don't
- Don't add a big Report button, a Tip button, a "N new works signed today" pill, or any counter or banner that doesn't help the next action.
- Don't blur anything that isn't a small chip over media. No blurred headers, tab bars or hero images.
- Don't use pure white, pure black, or a second accent color.
- Don't show buttons the backend can't honor (likes, reposts, comments, bell) or claim more than the signature proves.
- Don't put gradient text, colored side borders, nested cards or a kicker above a heading.

**Copy rules** (from `CLAUDE.md`): headings never end in a period; no em dashes; avoid "unlock", "elevate", "seamless", "whether you're A or B" and padded lists of three; never describe a feature as unbuilt or "coming soon" in shipped copy; every creator, not one niche (never "for photographers"); welcoming, never anti-anyone; protection in v1 is manual (a creator reports a copy and we help file the takedown), so never promise automatic scanning.
