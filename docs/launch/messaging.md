# How we talk about Emera

The playbook for every line a stranger reads: GitHub, site, store text, bios, posts, ads, emails. Facts come from `PRODUCT.md`; this file decides which ones lead and how they sound.

## The position

**Emera protects what you create** (Roy, 2026-11-23: protection is the main hook and the front door, not one slide).

Creators get copied, reposted and resold every day, and AI made it free. Emera is a fair place to make things and grow: we find the copies and take them down for you, the people here are real, and nobody can buy their way to the top.

The offer (Roy, 2026-11-26): **free protection**. Sign in, turn it on, no card. Paid plans watch more and you keep 80% of money we recover (50% on Free). Lead CTAs with "Get free protection".

Category: the open source social network (`brand.line`). That is the label on the box. The reason to open it is "we protect what you make", and "real people" is the proof that this is a fair place.

## The ladder

Every surface climbs it in this order and stops when space runs out.

| Step | Job | Say it like this |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Hook | The win the reader wants, in their words | "We protect what you create" |
| 2. Proof | How it is true, in one concrete line | "We find the copies and take them down for you" |
| 3. Contrast | The fair place, short and punchy | "Real people only. Reach can't be bought" |
| 4. Trust | Why to believe us | "Signed from the first upload", "open source", "run as a nonprofit" |

Trust is the last step, never the first. "Nonprofit", "open source" and "in the open" make a skeptic believe the hook; they do not make anyone want it.

## Three pillars, never more on one surface

| Pillar | The line | The proof behind it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Protected | "We find the copies and take them down for you" | scans, takedowns, Taken-down receipts, Emera Protect plans |
| Real people | "People are marked as people. Bots say what they are" | free verified-human mark, bot labels |
| Earned, not bought | "Reach can't be bought" | no ads or paid boosts in the feed, public ranking code, a reason on every post |

## Sound

- Short, present tense, sure of itself. Write it like the launch already happened.
- A concrete picture beats an adjective: "signed by the person who made it" beats "authentic".
- Benefit before mechanism. Say what the reader gets, then (if there is room) how.
- One idea per line. If a line has "and" twice, it is two lines.
- Name the problem, never a platform. Welcoming, not a rival pitch.

## Never lead with, never say

| Don't | Why | Say instead |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AT Protocol, decentralized, federated, PDS | Plumbing; nobody joins for it | "Take your account and followers with you, any time" (only if there is room) |
| "Post once. Protected everywhere, run as a nonprofit, in the open" | Filler stacked on filler; no picture, no reason to care | the ladder above |
| "share your work", "platform", "community-driven" | Every network says it | "what you make", "real people" |
| "verified" for every account | Not true: the human mark is optional | "people are marked as people" |
| "fact-checked", "100% real", "guaranteed" | The badge says only what was checked | "who posted it, and when" |
| unlock, elevate, seamless, em dashes, "whether you're A or B" | AI tells | plain words |

## Before it ships

1. Read only the first 8 words. Would a stranger know why Emera is different? No: rewrite the hook.
2. Does any line open with plumbing (protocol, nonprofit, open source)? Move it to the end.
3. Is every claim true today? Check `PRODUCT.md` and the app, not the roadmap.
4. Would a launched competitor print this sentence? No: it is a note for Roy, not copy.
