# Launch content

Written 2026-11-13. Drafts only; nothing is posted or sent. All of it filmable or writable by the founder alone.

Rules baked in: lead with the strongest true thing; never open with a zero or a miss; no numbers we do not have; protection is "we stand behind you when your work is copied" and the "Report a copy" flow, never "we watch for copies"; no rival is named negatively; screens use only Roy's own work or credited CC0 images, and invented demo creators with invented handles (`CLAUDE.md`). A video marked NEEDS cannot be filmed until that part of the product works on production.

## 3 emails to the early list

Plain text, from the founder. Subject lines have no trailing period.

### Email 1: you are on the list (sent on sign-up, plus a hand-written follow-up in week 2)

Subject: You are on the Emera list

Hi,

You asked to hear when Emera opens. Thank you.

Emera is a free, nonprofit network for everyone who makes things. Every post carries proof of who made it, the feed's ranking is public code, and you own your account. When your work gets copied, you report it and we help you file the takedown.

I would like to know one thing: what do you make, and where does your work get copied? Reply to this email with a line or two. I read every reply, and your answer decides who gets invited first.

TechGuyver
Emera, where humanity comes together

Thank you,
TechGuyver

## Copy checks run

- No em dashes, no "unlock", "elevate", "seamless", no "whether you're A or B".
- Posts: every line checked for no trailing period or comma.
- No claim of automatic scanning, no unbuilt feature described as available. Items needing a built flow are marked NEEDS.
- Numbers used: 40,000 to 650,000 (TechCrunch headline, linked); about 6 cents per user per month (estimate in `docs/one-pager.md`, labelled as such). No Emera user or creator count appears anywhere.
