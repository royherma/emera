Answers to your questions (facts):
- Live EMERA marks in cl. 9, 38, 41, 45: none. Cl. 42: none for the word EMERA itself; Emera US Holdings' BLOCKENERGY (cl. 42) is energy-technology consulting and is not the word EMERA.
- Only live mark naming Emera in cl. 9/38/42 is Sinelec's EMERA S C-ITS PLATFORM (road-traffic telecom/software, Italy). Different field, but a word-mark owner in our classes. The EMERA part is the distinctive word.
- Emera Inc.'s own US EMERA filings were two class 35 applications, both cancelled. In the US it holds the name through use (common law) and through subsidiaries' marks, not a live registered EMERA word mark. Class 4/37/39/40 EMERA registrations: none found under the Emera Incorporated name; the energy marks sit with subsidiaries (39, 37, 42).
- Owner search matched only exact owner names containing "emera"; Emera Inc. could also file under another entity name or a logo mark with no word searchable. Not ruled out.

### Famous mark / dilution
- Fact: Emera Inc. is a TSX and NYSE listed utility (about 30k LinkedIn followers) and now trades on the NYSE (2025). US dilution (15 U.S.C. 1125(c)) needs a mark "widely recognized by the general consuming public of the United States".
- JUDGMENT: Emera is known to investors and to customers in Nova Scotia, Florida (Tampa Electric), New Mexico. It is not a household name nationwide the way a consumer brand is. A fame-based dilution claim looks weak. The realistic risk is likelihood of confusion or a complaint, not dilution, and a nonprofit creator network is far from an energy utility. Both are lawyer questions.
- JUDGMENT: Emera AI Inc. (see below) and Emera Group USA sharing the name with no known dispute suggests the name coexists across fields.
