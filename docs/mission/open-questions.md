# Open questions for the founder

Five answers only you can give. Each has a recommendation, and I will treat it as the answer if you say nothing.

| # | Question | Recommended answer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Is work made with generative AI welcome, and how is it shown? | Welcome and labeled. The maker declares it (or C2PA says it), the label states the fact, and origin never lowers reach. Banning it fights value 7 and cannot be enforced; hiding it breaks value 1. |
| 2 | Who votes on values and the payout split? | One verified human (Tier 2), one vote. Tier 1 can propose and comment. This only works if Tier 2 is free (value 2) and an ID-free route exists, so voting is not a paid or identity-gated privilege. |
| 3 | When does your control end? | Interim board with you on it until the first member election, no later than 12 months after public launch, no personal veto after that. The "never" lines go into the bylaws on day one, so the promise binds you too. |
| 4 | Is paid reach ever allowed? | No, in the default feed. Change the one-pager's "declared promotion" line to apply only to third-party feeds members chose. This is the one place the current docs and this draft disagree. |
| 5 | How much does legal escalation (helping creators sue) get? | A named line of 10% of quarterly surplus [assumption], cases chosen by the board by public criteria, never promised to every creator. Value 3 says "fund", and a bounded fund keeps it honest. |
